# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in DenseTopo-UNet respectful and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive technical criticism constructively.
- Focus discussion on reproducible evidence and project goals.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and learn from them.
- Respect privacy, data-governance rules, and attribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly misrepresenting experimental evidence, provenance, or authorship.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional research setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. They may issue a correction request, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban according to the severity and persistence of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainers' GitHub profiles or repository moderation channels. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and provide a fair response to the extent possible.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
